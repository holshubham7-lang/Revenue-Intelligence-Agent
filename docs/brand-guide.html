Do / Don't

Quick reference for every surface.
Do
  • Use accent #34744E for the single primary CTA
  • Let neutrals & the off-white base carry layout
  • Keep the logo on clear, quiet backgrounds
  • Pair icon chips with text labels
  • Use accent-dark #2D6042 on hover states
Don't
  • Make green the dominant page background
  • Stretch, recolor, or silhouette the logo
  • Call the product "RIA" or "RevAgent"
  • Rely on color alone for status
  • Ship without a visible focus ring
